The Problem

Healthcare staffing in India still operates through fragmented and informal systems. Clinics struggle to quickly find trusted professionals, while doctors and nurses face unreliable opportunities and payment insecurity.

Reliance on unstructured chats for critical staffing.

Last-Minute Chaos

Zero speed when an urgent cover is needed.

The Solution

Medrova connects verified doctors, nurses, and healthcare professionals with clinics and hospitals. Every professional is manually verified before activation.
