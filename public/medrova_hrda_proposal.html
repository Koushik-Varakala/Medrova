The Solution

How Medrova Works

Medrova is a B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that acts as an intelligent matchmaking engine between verified doctors and hospitals. Medrova never touches the doctor's salary.

1

Doctor Signs Up (100% Free, Forever)

HRDA-verified doctors create a profile with their specialization, availability, preferred locations, and NMC registration. There is zero cost to the doctor — no subscription, no commission, no hidden fees.

2

Hospital Posts a Shift or Job (Free to Post)

Hospitals and clinics post their staffing needs — locum night shifts, weekend coverage, or permanent positions. Posting is completely free. Medrova's algorithm matches them with qualified, available doctors.

3

Hospital Uses 1 Credit to Confirm (₹400–₹750 per Credit)

When the hospital finds the right doctor, they spend 1 Medrova Credit to unlock the doctor's contact information and confirm the shift. This is the only point where money is involved — paid by the hospital, never the doctor.

4

Doctor Gets Paid Directly by the Hospital

The doctor receives their full salary directly from the hospital via UPI, bank transfer, or cash — exactly as they do today. Medrova never deducts any commission from the doctor's earnings.

🛡️

The HRDA Guarantee: 100% Direct Payment

Currently, doctors are paid directly by the hospital (via cash or UPI) with zero commission taken by Medrova. By keeping Medrova out of the salary transaction in Phase 1, we ensure doctors get their full pay instantly. (Future Roadmap: We are also developing an optional Bank-Grade Escrow system for automated payouts).

Partnership Benefits

What's in it for HRDA?

This partnership is designed to strengthen HRDA's mission, protect its members, and create a sustainable funding source for the association's future.

1

Total Financial Safety for All Members

HRDA's core mission is protecting doctors' rights. Medrova eliminates the #1 problem your members face: payment scams and delays on WhatsApp locum groups. By endorsing Medrova, HRDA provides its doctors with a verified, trusted platform where hospitals are pre-vetted. In Phase 1, doctors are paid their full salary directly by the hospital with zero commission. In Phase 2, we will introduce automated Escrow payouts.

2

Exclusive Healthcare Market Intelligence

Medrova will generate unprecedented data: average locum rates by district, hospital demand patterns, doctor satisfaction ratings, and which facilities are repeat offenders for late payments. HRDA receives exclusive, anonymized quarterly reports to power government lobbying, media campaigns, and policy advocacy with hard data instead of anecdotes.

3

Community Revenue Share (5% of Platform Revenue)

As the official partner, HRDA receives a 5% share of all gross platform fees collected from hospitals. This is entirely passive income for the association — no investment, no operational burden. HRDA can allocate these funds to medical conferences, legal advocacy, member welfare programs, or any initiative the association sees fit.
