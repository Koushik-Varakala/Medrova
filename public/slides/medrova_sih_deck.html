Medrova: Zero-Commission Locum Doctor Shift Management System
Smart India
Hackathon 2026

Problem:

Hospitals and clinics face sudden doctor absenteeism, relying on unverified WhatsApp groups with a 35–40% dropout rate and zero credential auditing. This exposes facilities to quackery, legal penalties under NMC Act 2019 (Sec 34), and vicarious malpractice liability (Supreme Court: Savita Garg case).

Proposed Solution :

  • Mandatory Credential Audit Wall: Physicians must submit verified MBBS/MD degrees, State Council cards (TSMC/APMC/NMC), and photo ID. Unverified accounts are blocked at the engine level.
  • Deterministic Shift Lifecycle Engine: Bookings transition through an auditable, code-enforced pipeline: Open → Applied → Confirmed → In-Progress → Completed → Settled.
  • PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS): Hospital rotas and doctor data are segregated at the database engine layer (DPDP Act 2023 compliant).

Our Idea :

Medrova is a secure medical software platform featuring a zero-commission peer settlement model and fail-safe credential gating. It connects verified facilities directly with licensed physicians within a 5–25 km geo-radius, cutting fill latency to under 30 minutes.

Innovation/Uniqueness:

  • Zero-Commission Architecture: Avoids the working-capital collapse of traditional agencies (Nomad Health 2026 pivot); facilities settle 100% directly with clinicians.
  • Real-Time Shift State Machine: Unlike static job portals (Naukri, Docthub) taking 4–6 weeks for resume reviews, Medrova executes instant same-day shift dispatch.
  • Kernel-Level Multi-Tenancy: Security enforced inside PostgreSQL engine policies.
  • Empirically Validated Pilot: Tested with 4 verified doctors and 2 facilities (including Precise Hospital).

Software Stack & Architecture

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Hackathon 2026

Feasibility

  • Serverless Scalability: Built on Next.js 14 and Supabase PostgreSQL with zero dedicated physical server overhead, auto-scaling from 10 to 10,000 concurrent shift queries.
  • Zero App Store Friction: Progressive mobile web client runs directly in any mobile or desktop browser without requiring downloads or device storage.
  • Automated State Pipeline: Enforces a deterministic 6-stage lifecycle (Open → Settled) using PostgreSQL ACID transactions, eliminating race conditions.
  • Zero-Dependency Credential Gate: Validates TSMC, APMC, and NMC registration numbers directly against official state registries without costly HR outsourcing.
  • Pilot-Tested Infrastructure: Validated in real clinical environments across Hyderabad.

Commercial feasibility

  • Market Validation: High demand — India has over 43,000 private clinics and secondary hospitals facing chronic doctor absenteeism and a 70% specialist deficit (NITI Aayog).
  • Float-Free Unit Economics: Eliminates the fatal agency working-capital trap (the Nomad Health 2026 pivot); facilities pay clinicians directly (0% commission) while buying flat software credits (&8377;750/booking vs. &8377;2,500–&8377;5,000 broker cuts).
  • Technical Readiness: 100% live & functional at https://medrova.in with active authentication, database RLS, and Cashfree payment integration.
  • Success Likelihood: High (>90%) — clinics save &8377;15,000–&8377;35,000 monthly; doctors receive 100% of their earnings immediately via UPI.

Challenges

  • Cold-Start Doctor Supply: Initial localized doctor liquidity constraints within specific 5–10 km municipal wards during early launch weeks.
  • Medical Credential Forgery: Risk of fraudulent or altered state council registration cards and certificates being submitted by unvetted applicants.
  • Last-Minute Doctor Cancellations: Risk of a confirmed doctor failing to report for duty during an emergency shift, leaving an ICU or casualty ward unstaffed.
  • Data Leakage Between Rivals: Competing private hospital groups attempting to access or scrape each other's staffing rotas, clinician contacts, and pay rates.

Strategy:

  • Hyper-Local Cluster Rollout: Launching dense, zone-by-zone medical clusters (e.g., Gachibowli, Banjara Hills, Kukatpally) before citywide expansion to guarantee fast shift fill rates.
  • Dual-Layer KYC & Council Cross-Check: Manual document audit cross-referenced against public Telangana State Medical Council (TSMC) registries, with DigiLocker API integration.
  • Geofenced Check-in & Reliability Scoring: Real-time GPS check-in at hospital premises on arrival; clinicians with repeat cancellations lose booking priority.
  • Kernel-Level PostgreSQL RLS Isolation: Enforcing tenant isolation at the database engine layer, making cross-hospital data breaches impossible (DPDP Act 2023).
